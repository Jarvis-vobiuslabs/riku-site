The Story

A girl who closes dimensional rifts
every single night at 2 AM.

Dimensional rifts tear open above Tokyo every single night. Monsters pour through. And seventeen-year-old Riku is the only person on earth who can close them — because she's the one who accidentally opened the first one, and lost her mother inside it.

She's been fighting alone for three months. She trusts no one. Her katana, Akagiri, has absorbed the energy of every monster it's killed. Last week it started glowing red. Then a secret organization called SHINKEN came looking for her. And something ancient came through the next Rift — and called her by name.
