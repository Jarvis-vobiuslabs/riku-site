The Story

Tokyo, 2 AM.
Every single night.

Dimensional rifts tear open above the city. Monsters pour through. And every night, seventeen-year-old Riku is the only one who closes them.

She's been doing this for three months — since her mother disappeared into the Rift she accidentally opened. Riku fights alone. She trusts no one.

But a secret organization has been watching her. And something ancient is coming through the next Rift. Something that knows her name.
