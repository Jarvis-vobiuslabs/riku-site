The Story

Three months of fighting alone.
Every night at 2 AM.

Dimensional rifts tear open above Tokyo. Monsters pour through. And every night, seventeen-year-old Riku is the only one who closes them.

She's been doing this since her mother disappeared into the Rift she accidentally opened. Riku fights alone. She trusts no one. That's her first mistake.

A secret organization has been watching her for weeks. Something ancient is coming through the next Rift. And it already knows her name.
